# Renal Function Reference Guide

## Overview
Renal functional markers evaluate the glomerular filtration, tubular secretory, and electrolyte balance capabilities of the kidneys.

## Key Parameters & Clinical Context

### Serum Creatinine & eGFR
- **Serum Creatinine**: Typically 0.6–1.2 mg/dL. Generated at a relatively constant rate proportional to muscle mass.
- **Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR)**: Mathematically derived filtration capacity based on serum creatinine, age, and sex. Values > 90 mL/min/1.73m² represent normal baseline filtration.
- **Contextual Note**: Acute variations indicate possible hemodynamic shifts, acute kidney injury, or medication effects.

### Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN) and BUN/Creatinine Ratio
- **BUN Reference Range**: 7–20 mg/dL.
- **BUN/Creatinine Ratio**: Typically 10:1 to 20:1. Elevated ratios (>20:1) frequently signal prerenal azotemia or hypovolemia, while intrinsic renal injury generally manifests with proportionate elevations.

### Serum Electrolytes in Renal Assessment
- Potassium clearance is predominantly renal; hyperkalemia (>5.0 mEq/L) warrants immediate medical review in renal impairment.

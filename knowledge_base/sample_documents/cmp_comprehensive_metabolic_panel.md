# Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) Reference Guide

## Overview
A Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) evaluates electrolyte equilibrium, glucose metabolism, renal filtration parameters, and hepatic synthetic capacity from blood serum or plasma.

## Key Parameters & Clinical Context

### Fasting Blood Glucose
- **Biological Role**: Primary cellular energy substrate circulating in the bloodstream.
- **Reference Range**: Normal fasting intervals are typically 70–99 mg/dL. Fasting glucose between 100–125 mg/dL is categorized as impaired fasting glucose, while 126 mg/dL or higher on repeat testing requires clinical evaluation for diabetes mellitus.
- **Contextual Note**: Serum glucose levels fluctuate with nutritional status, fasting duration, acute hormonal counter-regulation, and medications.

### Serum Creatinine
- **Biological Role**: End-product of muscle creatine phosphate catabolism excreted almost entirely by renal glomerular filtration.
- **Reference Range**: Commonly 0.6–1.2 mg/dL for adult males and 0.5–1.1 mg/dL for adult females, varying with muscle mass and age.
- **Contextual Note**: Elevation above the laboratory's reported reference interval suggests decreased glomerular filtration rate, but must be interpreted alongside baseline measurements, hydration status, and urine output.

### Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)
- **Biological Role**: Nitrogenous metabolic byproduct synthesized by hepatocytes during amino acid deamination.
- **Reference Range**: Typically 7–20 mg/dL.
- **Contextual Note**: BUN is influenced by protein intake, hydration state, and gastrointestinal bleeding in addition to renal clearance.

### Serum Electrolytes (Sodium, Potassium, Chloride, Carbon Dioxide)
- **Sodium (Na)**: 135–145 mEq/L (or mmol/L); regulates extracellular fluid osmolarity.
- **Potassium (K)**: 3.5–5.0 mEq/L; essential for cardiac electrochemical membrane potentials.
- **Calcium**: 8.5–10.2 mg/dL; regulated by parathyroid hormone and vitamin D.

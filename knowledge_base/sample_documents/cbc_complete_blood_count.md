# Complete Blood Count (CBC) Reference Guide

## Overview
A Complete Blood Count (CBC) is a routine laboratory test that measures various cellular components circulating in peripheral blood. It evaluates cellular quantity, morphological indices, and relative proportions of distinct cell types.

## Key Parameters & Clinical Context

### Hemoglobin (Hb)
- **Biological Role**: An iron-rich protein in red blood cells responsible for transporting oxygen from lungs to peripheral tissues and returning carbon dioxide.
- **Reference Range Variations**: Typically printed as 13.0–17.0 g/dL for adult males and 12.0–15.5 g/dL for adult non-pregnant females, but exact cutoff intervals depend strictly on the executing laboratory's assay instruments and calibration.
- **Contextual Note**: A measurement below the laboratory reference interval indicates diminished circulating hemoglobin concentration. However, this numerical finding alone does not establish underlying etiology (such as iron deficiency, hemoglobinopathy, acute blood loss, or chronic renal disease) and requires clinical physician correlation.

### White Blood Cell Count (WBC)
- **Biological Role**: Cellular components of the immune defense mechanism responding to infectious, inflammatory, or hematological stimuli.
- **Reference Range**: Commonly 4.0–11.0 × 10^3/µL (or K/uL).
- **Contextual Note**: Results above the reference range are known as leukocytosis and can occur in physiological stress, bacterial infection, tissue trauma, or inflammatory states. Values below reference intervals (leukopenia) require comprehensive hematologic evaluation.

### Platelets
- **Biological Role**: Disc-shaped cell fragments essential for primary hemostasis and thrombus formation.
- **Reference Range**: Typically 150–450 × 10^3/µL (K/uL).
- **Contextual Note**: Thrombocytopenia (values below reported range) or thrombocytosis (values above reported range) reflect altered thrombopoiesis or peripheral consumption.

### Red Blood Cell Indices (MCV, MCH, MCHC)
- **MCV (Mean Corpuscular Volume)**: Measures average erythrocyte volume (80–100 fL).
- **MCH & MCHC**: Measure average hemoglobin weight and concentration per red cell.
- **Contextual Note**: Used in hematology to classify red cell size morphology (microcytic, normocytic, macrocytic).
